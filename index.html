encyclopedia

the same thought,
upon seeing my own
reflection seven
years old: opening
the new Encarta at lunch,

finally the future
is arriving, here in the
tropics, and then,
pyramids, carbon
cycle outlasting generations
of oceans and continents,
the first metal
mirrors, paths over land
bridges following coasts,

paint from arsenic to lead
to acrylic, the last ice
age and the Ordovician,
satellites, Pluto, we will

reach it some day, a space
torus, spinning its own
gravity, spinning seven
eras of luck, the difficult
birth of the moon; my brain
behind my foreign face
the monitor and its tomorrow,

when we can reach
anyone a world away,
today, today, is this

how it really is? I have been
born. A vast
field before me,
after me, around me, a
pearl inside an oyster’s
body, sliding around.
where or when

am I? the light from who
knows where blurring
my own fingers. sleeping

later, I will dream
of meeting merfolk outfitted
with custom genes, sitting with
my grandmothers,
alive again, somewhere
spiralling in Andromeda
not knowing that name.
